Research

From predictions to
evaluated explanations.

My research examines uncertainty, explanation reliability, and data-efficient learning in medical and industrial computer vision.

Research themes

Uncertainty & explanations

Evaluate whether explanations reflect the features a model uses, and how confidence estimates behave when predictions fail.

Medical image understanding

Study segmentation, pathology localisation, and the relationship between medical images and generated radiology reports.

Learning with limited labels

Develop annotation-efficient and adaptive models for medical segmentation and heterogeneous industrial quality control.
